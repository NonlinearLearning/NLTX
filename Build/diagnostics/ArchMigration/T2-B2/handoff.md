## Remaining partial work and caller disposition

The source is not an Arch-only production cutover yet:

- `EntityUuidIssuer` owns the issued-history set, but history continuity across candidate sessions is not guaranteed: the default registry constructor creates its own issuer, and `LoadedWorldSession` still accepts or creates a registry. The corrected ownership decision is recorded above and in `dependency-signature-freeze.md`.
- `EntityIdentityRegistry.cs` still maps `EntityUuid ↔ RuntimeEntityHandle`; it has not been switched to `(session token, Arch.Entity)`.
- `LoadedWorldSession` still exposes `EntityRuntime`; `EntityRuntime.cs`, `ComponentAccess.cs`, `ComponentStore.cs`, and `RuntimeEntityHandle.cs` remain active production APIs. No compatibility claim is made for them.
- `WorldStorageRoot.cs` still accepts `EntityRuntime`; `TileEntityStore.cs`, `TileEntityRecord.cs`, `EntitySlotStore.cs`, and `TileEntityBindingComponent.cs` have not been moved to token-validated Arch Entity projections.
- `WorldSessionRestoreState` remains an exposed aggregate of component references. The singleton stores those same objects, but call sites have not yet been converted to direct singleton component access; the owner-state cutover is incomplete.
- Independent runtime constructors remain in `src/NSSLC.Tools.Simulation/RuntimeNpcStore.cs` (lines 79, 413) and `RuntimePlayerStore.cs` (lines 38, 402).
- Network and lifecycle callers continue to use legacy handles/operations. Their exact per-file symbols and owning projects are in the committed `caller-inventory.csv`; B2.0 records the T3/T4/T5 handoff split.
